Welcome to your own music television

Step 02

Where's your music?

KTV reads which songs you like from a music service, then plays their music videos. Start with one; you can connect more later in the control room.

Source

Connect Spotify

KTV reads your songs through a free Spotify app that you create. It takes a couple of minutes, and the account that creates the app needs Spotify Premium.

  1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard and log in.
  2. Click Create app. The name and description can be anything.
  3. Under Redirect URIs, add exactly
  4. Where it asks which APIs you plan to use, tick Web API, then save.
  5. Open the app's Settings and paste its Client ID and Client secret below.
